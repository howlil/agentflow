---
title: Release Engineering
description: Repeatable builds, CI gates, promotion and safe production transitions.
---

Use `release-engineering` when the unresolved decision concerns CI/CD, artifact identity, environment promotion, rollout, rollback or migration sequencing. It consumes test proof and operational health objectives; it does not replace their owners.

Output: executable delivery changes and revision-bound evidence in existing task/release records. First establish the candidate, target, compatibility window and recovery mechanism. Claim deployment only after observing target execution and critical behavior.

[Read the complete `release-engineering` instructions](/reference/release-engineering/).
