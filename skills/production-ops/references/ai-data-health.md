# AI and data production-ops

Use when model, prompt, retrieval or data semantics affect this specialist’s responsibility.

Monitor the accepted business outcome and material failure slices, not only HTTP success. Identify quality degradation, freshness, drift, latency and cost signals that support a real decision. Distinguish model, prompt, retrieval/data and infrastructure changes during diagnosis. Define a human/manual or deterministic fallback when required; give alert ownership and an actionable response. Version observations and avoid sensitive payloads in telemetry. Replay or backfill only with the system owner and repeat-safety guarantees. Request release-engineering rollback/roll-forward and verify both service health and output/data quality afterward.
