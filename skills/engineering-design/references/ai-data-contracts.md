# AI and data engineering-design

Use when model, prompt, retrieval or data semantics affect this specialist’s responsibility.

Model authoritative data ownership, provenance, freshness, schema compatibility and replay/backfill semantics. For learned or generative behavior, compare a simpler baseline and define permitted error, human fallback, latency and cost constraints from evidence. Identify model/prompt/index/data versions and authority boundaries. Treat retrieved content and generated output as untrusted at privileged sinks. Route quality proof to test-engineering, tool/data authority to security-review, promotion to release-engineering and quality monitoring to production-ops. Do not assume a model change preserves behavior.
