# Code of conduct

Agentflow welcomes respectful, technically grounded collaboration in repository Issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project-managed discussion spaces.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and code constructively; challenge reasoning without attacking people.
- Be considerate of different experience levels and backgrounds.
- Keep reports and reviews relevant, actionable and free of harassment.
- Respect personal privacy; do not share anyone's private data or credentials.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, targeted insults, doxxing, and repeated disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

## Moderation and reporting

Maintainers may moderate repository discussions, hide or remove inappropriate content, close abusive threads or restrict participation using GitHub's available controls. Moderation decisions should focus on documented conduct and the safety of participants.

Use GitHub's built-in content reporting tools for harassment, privacy violations or abuse when available. For a non-sensitive repository conduct concern, open a [GitHub Issue](https://github.com/howlil/agentflow/issues/new) describing the moderation request without exposing personal or sensitive details. A public Issue is **not** a confidential reporting channel. Do not include identifying details about victims or confidential correspondence there.

Security disclosures follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not public conduct reports. This document does not promise a private contact address or a fixed response deadline.
